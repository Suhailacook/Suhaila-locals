Overview

Discover الحوح (Houh) - a delicate, centuries-old Aqabawi sweet that melts on your tongue. This traditional pastry is meticulously crafted with thin, crepe-like sheets of dough layered with ghee, then soaked in aromatic rose and cardamom-infused syrup. Each plate is a culinary masterpiece prepared fresh with authentic techniques passed down through generations. Perfect for special occasions, celebrations, or whenever you crave an authentic taste of Aqaba's rich culinary heritage.

What Makes Our Houh Special

  • Thin, Delicate Layers: Hand-prepared crepe-like sheets that are translucent and melt in your mouth, never thick or doughy.
  • Authentic Syrup: Aromatic blend infused with rose water and cardamom, creating a perfect balance of sweet and fragrant flavors.
  • Premium Ghee: Brushed between each layer for authentic taste and texture.
  • Fresh Preparation: Made to order using time-honored Aqabawi techniques.
  • Perfect Soaking: Carefully soaked to achieve the ideal softness without becoming soggy.

How It's Made

Step 1: Prepare the Batter

Create a very thin batter from flour, water, and a pinch of salt - the foundation for delicate layers.

Step 2: Cook the Sheets

Carefully cook each sheet on a hot surface until it becomes translucent and forms delicate, lace-like patterns.

Step 3: Layer with Ghee

Stack the sheets, brushing authentic ghee between each layer for richness and tradition.

Step 4: Soak in Syrup

Soak the entire stack in aromatic syrup infused with rose water and cardamom for perfect flavor absorption.
