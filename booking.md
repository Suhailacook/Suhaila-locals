# Booking Suhaila Locals

Suhaila Locals accepts booking requests for local experiences in Aqaba, Jordan. Visitors should use the reservation page, choose the experience, and provide the requested date, group size, and contact details. The team should confirm availability and any final logistics directly with the visitor.

## Booking links

- [Reserve an experience](Reservation.html)
- [Contact Suhaila Locals](contact-Us.html)
- [WhatsApp contact](https://wa.me/message/XW5T6AR3LHINN1)

## Important booking facts

The cooking class costs 30 JOD per person and lasts 3 hours. The walking and food tour costs 20 JOD per person and lasts 2.5 hours. The dining experience costs 15 JOD per person and lasts 2.5 hours. The glass boat costs 25 JOD per hour per boat for up to 6 guests. Houh delivery costs 10 JOD per plate, requires a minimum order of 3 plates, is delivered within Aqaba, and needs at least 24 hours' advance notice. The online cooking class costs 20 USD per person and takes place live over Zoom.

Prices and availability may change. Treat the individual experience page and confirmation from Suhaila Locals as the current source for a booking decision.

## Agent-readable booking behavior

An assistant may explain the experiences and link to the reservation page, but it should not claim that a date is available or that a booking is confirmed unless the website or Suhaila Locals has explicitly confirmed it. Do not bypass the normal form, consent, or contact process.
