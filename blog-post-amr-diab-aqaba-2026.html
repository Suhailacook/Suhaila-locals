Amr Diab in Aqaba: Your Concert Guide for September 2026

Published on September 10, 2026 | By Suhaila Locals Team

Concert at a Glance

Amr Diab live in Aqaba
Friday, September 18, 2026
Ayla Golf Club, Aqaba
Doors and schedule: check the official ticket listing before you travel
Tickets are listed from JOD 90 on the official event page.

Aqaba is getting ready for a memorable night of Arabic pop as Amr Diab comes to the Red Sea city. Known for decades of hits and energetic live performances, the Egyptian superstar will perform at Ayla Golf Club on September 18, 2026.

What to Know Before You Go

The official event listing confirms the date and Ayla Golf Club venue. Ticket prices are listed from JOD 90, but availability, entry times, seating categories, and any age requirements can change. Buy through the official ticket link and keep your confirmation ready on your phone.

Ayla is a short drive from central Aqaba. Plan your transport in advance, especially if you are staying in the city center. A taxi or pre-arranged ride is usually the simplest option, and it is wise to agree on your return plan before the concert begins.

Make an Evening of It in Aqaba

Arrive early enough to enjoy Aqaba before the music starts. Take a walk along the waterfront, browse the local shops, or have dinner featuring Jordanian seafood and mezze. If you are staying overnight, the concert is a perfect reason to add a relaxed morning by the Red Sea the following day.

Local tip: September evenings can still feel warm. Wear comfortable shoes, bring a light layer for later at night, and keep your ticket, identification, and transport details together.

Enjoy the Night Responsibly

Large concerts are busiest when gates open and when the show ends. Follow the venue's security instructions, use official transport or a trusted taxi, and choose a clear meeting point if you are attending with friends. Most importantly, enjoy the music and the special atmosphere of Aqaba with respect for the city and its community.
