Use this prompt set once per month in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google Search features that show AI answers. Record the date, country/language, whether Suhaila Locals appeared, the cited source URL, competitors mentioned, and whether prices and experience details were accurate.

## What to improve after each review

If the answer is inaccurate, update the page that should state the fact plainly, then update `llms.txt` and `experiences.md` if the fact is part of the public business summary. If Suhaila Locals is absent but competitors are cited, compare the cited pages for direct answers, original local detail, freshness, author attribution, internal links, and third-party reputation. Do not publish unsupported claims or keyword-stuffed copy simply to influence an AI answer.

## Success metrics

Track citation rate separately from recommendation rate. A citation means a page was used as a source; a recommendation means Suhaila Locals appeared on the visitor's shortlist. Also track whether the assistant correctly understands the location, experience type, price, duration, and booking path.
