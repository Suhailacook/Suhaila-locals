Recipe Ingredients

This is the ingredient list we send before class — everything is available in most supermarkets, and substitutes are noted for anything harder to find outside Jordan.

🥙 Hummus

  • ✓ 2 cups cooked chickpeas (2 cans, drained)
  • ✓ ⅓ cup tahini
  • ✓ ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
  • ✓ 1–2 cloves garlic
  • ✓ 2–3 tbsp olive oil
  • ✓ ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ✓ Salt, to taste
  • ✓ 2–3 tbsp ice-cold water

🧆 Falafel

  • ✓ 1½ cups dried chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • ✓ ½ medium onion
  • ✓ 3–4 cloves garlic
  • ✓ ½ cup fresh parsley
  • ✓ ¼ cup fresh cilantro
  • ✓ 1 tsp cumin & 1 tsp coriander
  • ✓ ½ tsp cardamom (optional)
  • ✓ ½ tsp baking powder
  • ✓ Neutral oil, for frying
📌 Good to know: Falafel uses dried chickpeas soaked overnight, not canned — that's the one prep step to start a day ahead. Can't find tahini locally? Check the international aisle first; it's usually there. No fresh cilantro? Just leave it out, the parsley still carries the flavor.

Meet Agareed: Your Guide to Jordanian Cooking

Agareed

With decades of culinary experience passed down through generations, Agareed shares her passion for Jordanian cuisine and culture in every class — making an authentic recipe easy to follow, and genuinely fun to cook, even through a screen.

Why Take an Online Jordanian Cooking Class?

Not everyone can fly to Aqaba for a hands-on food experience — but you can still learn to cook real Jordanian food from someone who grew up making it. Our online Jordanian cooking class brings the same warmth and hospitality of an in-person lesson straight into your kitchen, wherever you live. It's a live, virtual cooking class, not a video course: you're cooking alongside Agareed in real time, asking questions as you go, and finishing the session with a dish you made with your own hands.

It's a popular choice for home cooks who want to learn Middle Eastern cooking online, for people planning a trip to Jordan who want a taste of the culture beforehand, and for anyone looking for a fun, hands-on virtual activity to do solo, as a couple, or with friends over Zoom.

How the Online Cooking Class Works

  1. Book your spot — choose a date, and we'll confirm a class time that works with your time zone.
  2. Get your ingredient list — sent in advance, with substitutes for ingredients that may be harder to find outside Jordan.
  3. Join live on Zoom — cook hummus and falafel from scratch, step by step, with Agareed guiding you in real time.
  4. Ask questions as you go — it's an interactive class, not a recording, so you can pause, ask, and get help exactly when you need it.
  5. Keep the recipe — a PDF recipe guide is sent after the session so you can cook it again anytime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any special equipment?

No. You just need a stovetop, a mixing bowl, a food processor or blender for the hummus, and a device to join Zoom. Everything else is explained in the ingredient list sent before class.

What if I can't find an ingredient where I live?

The ingredient list includes global supermarket substitutes, so you can join the class no matter which country you're cooking from.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. Agareed guides you step by step in real time, so it works for complete beginners as well as experienced home cooks.

Can I book a private class for my family or friends?

Yes, private group sessions can be arranged. Contact us directly via WhatsApp or email to set one up.

What time zone are classes held in?

Classes are scheduled around Aqaba, Jordan time (GMT+3), but after booking we coordinate a start time that works for your time zone.

What do I get after the class ends?

A PDF recipe guide for hummus and falafel is sent to you after the session, so you can cook the dishes again anytime.
